🤖 A I — Build & Battle 🤖

Pick a vehicle 🚗🐜⛵🚂 (scroll / triggers), bolt on weapons, then fight each other! — knock out all 10 ❤️.
Player 1 (pad 1): stick/arrows move, A = place & SHOOT (hold), triggers or X·Y switch weapon, B remove, Start next.
Player 2 (pad 2): stick/WASD move, F = place & SHOOT (hold), triggers switch weapon, G remove, Start next.
Ranged weapons fire while you hold SHOOT. Spikes, knives & the 💎 diamond sword hurt on contact!
