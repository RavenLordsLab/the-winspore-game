MINECRAFT

Creator Mode + Mobs

WASD move   Mouse look   Space fly up   Shift fly down
Left Click break   Right Click place / ride boat / trade villager
1-9 choose item   Q/E cycle   F exit boat   Esc menu

Mobs: Skeletons shoot arrows. Zombies chase and bump. Creepers explode (3-block radius). Villagers trade — right-click them and they hand you random blocks!

Tip: Build houses/hideouts with blocks. Pick "Boat" from the hotbar and right-click water to drop one, then right-click the boat to ride it.

Gamepad (controller 1 only): Left stick move   Right stick look
A fly up / interact   B fly down   RT place   LT break   LB/RB cycle blocks
