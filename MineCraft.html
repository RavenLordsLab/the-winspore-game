What to do
Find the village. The villagers are brown people with big rectangle noses, and they say “Hrrm!”
Talk to them and open the chests in their houses — they hand you blocks so you can build.
Say hi to the cows and pigs.
At night zombies, skeletons, creepers and MUTANT ZOMBIES come out, so build a shelter and seal the door before dark!
